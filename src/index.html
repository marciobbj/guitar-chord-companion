HOW TO USE THE CUSTOM HARMONY MAP

In CUSTOM HARMONY MAP (ALL CHORDS) mode, you can toggle which degrees of the key are visible on the fretboard and independently pick the Inversion (`Root`, `1st Inv`, `2nd Inv`, `3rd Inv`) and Position for each chord.

Each degree has its own color badge (`I` through `vii`), and shared notes between multiple chords are highlighted in dark/yellow showing all degrees that share that fret (e.g. `I+iii`), making it easy to visualize common tones and voice leading across the fretboard.

  • AUTO VOICE-LEAD: Automatically selects inversions for every chord in the key so they stay in the same fretboard neighborhood with minimal hand movement.
  • SINGLE CHORD FULL NECK: Switches back to the classic view showing all occurrences of a single clicked chord across all 18 frets.
