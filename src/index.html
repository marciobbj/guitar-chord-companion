HOW TO READ THE FRETBOARD (INVERSIONS)

When you select a chord (e.g. "V7"), the fretboard above displays all notes of that chord across the entire fretboard.

In the OPEN column (open strings), strings always keep their tuning visible (e.g. `E · R` or `A · 5`) and are highlighted in blue (Root) or yellow (Chord Tone) when they belong to the selected chord.

An inversion is simply the same chord played with a note other than the Root (R) as the lowest note (the bass).

  • Root Position: The lowest note is the Root (R).
  • 1st Inversion: The lowest note is the Third (3 or b3).
  • 2nd Inversion: The lowest note is the Fifth (5 or b5).
  • 3rd Inversion: The lowest note is the Seventh (7 or b7).
